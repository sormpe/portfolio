# Homepage surface brief

Scope: the single-page portfolio homepage. Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience, voice, and content constraints live in PRODUCT.md.

Job: a person hiring a consultant or a recruiter decides whether Peter fits, then gets in touch. Action: email, in the first viewport and again after Work and at the close.

Proof: five real projects grouped under Frontend, Backend, and Cloud. Layer mapping, confirmed by the owner: Pricing all three; University and Education Platform frontend and backend; High-Traffic frontend and cloud; Matching Tool backend and cloud.

Headline wording approved by the owner. How people hire him stays deliberately unstated. Work has a By layer and By date switch, and Experience lists each employer's projects, both requested by the owner.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A conventional developer portfolio at very high craft, chosen on purpose as the category standard. It refuses a generic stat row, eyebrows, and tag walls, and leads with range rather than a name.

OWN-WORLD: White and light grey section grounds, #0e0e0e ink, one vermilion accent, one full-width ink Contact close, self-hosted Schibsted Grotesk with heavy tight headings, 1px rules. Flat, precise, no cards. Quality bar: Brittany Chiang, Josh Comeau, Linear.

STORY: The visitor learns he covers frontend, backend, and cloud on new and existing systems, sees proof per layer, confirms history, then emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The statement is the largest element, left, across about two thirds of the width. Name and title sit above it, availability below, then Email me as the primary button and See the work as secondary. A small portrait sits right. Nav on top with a distinct Contact control.

FORM: Category standard, the owner's chosen standing exit, outside the ordered list. seed:1c07b6a0b61eba2f. Signature interaction: each project links to its entries in its other layers, and arriving highlights the entry once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
